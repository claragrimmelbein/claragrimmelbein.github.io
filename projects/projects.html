The Limits of Temporal Prediction in Short-Form Video 2025 – 2026

Distinguished Major thesis. A custom Transformer encoder in PyTorch trained on 7 years of my own TikTok interaction data (114k+ events) to predict watch time — benchmarked against linear and mean baselines, with SHAP attribution to diagnose overfitting. Reached 7.97s MAE on high-frequency usage and found a predictability gradient that scales with intensity, which I read as evidence for variable reinforcement over engagement-signal optimization.

MediAIte: LLM Mediation for Legal Disputes 2026

An extension of DeepMind's Habermas Machine for an AI & Law seminar, applied to a mock premises-liability dispute. Swapped in a custom Poe-hosted mediator model against a GPT-4 baseline; the pipeline runs chain-of-thought generation, LLM ranking, and Schulze aggregation across opinion and critique rounds to converge on a statement both parties accept.
